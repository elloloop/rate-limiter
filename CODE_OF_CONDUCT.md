# Code of Conduct

This project expects direct, technical, respectful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific about bugs, risks, tradeoffs, and evidence.
- Assume good intent while still asking for proof when correctness matters.
- Keep review comments focused on the code, tests, docs, and operational
  impact.
- Respect maintainer decisions about project scope and non-goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Public disclosure of private security reports before a fix or maintainer
  response window.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or reviews after a maintainer has asked for a
  topic to move elsewhere.
- Submitting work under a license or ownership claim that conflicts with the
  project license or CLA.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues, reject contributions,
or block accounts when behavior harms contributors or project safety. Serious
security or legal concerns may be handled privately.
